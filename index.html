Admission Routes & Fee Structure

Undergraduate admissions are allocated through three transparent routes.

~45% Seats

KCET (Karnataka CET)

₹1.2L – ₹1.3L / year

Government-regulated fees for Karnataka students through KEA counseling.

  • KEA Code: E007
  • Allocates ~45% of seats
  • Subsidized merit fee
~30% Seats

COMEDK UGET

₹3.1L – ₹3.3L / year

National merit entrance test open to students from across India.

  • COMEDK Code: E035
  • Allocates ~30% of seats
  • Standard merit fee
Direct Entry

Management Quota

₹3.0L – ₹5.5L / year

Direct institutional admission based on 10+2/PUC merit and branch demand.

  • Min. 50% aggregate in PCM
  • Direct campus counseling
  • Guaranteed seat allotment
