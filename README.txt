Overview:
A clean, responsive college website developed purely in semantic HTML5 and CSS3 (no frameworks, no external JS). Inspired by premier educational institution portals, designed with clean structure and student-friendly first-year assignment code standards.

2. Hero Section: Overview banner, call-to-action buttons, verified Google rating badge (4.2★ / 3.9K reviews), and campus imagery.
3. Statistics Bar: Key metrics (45+ Years legacy, 30-Acre campus, 20 B.E. programs, ₹57 LPA highest package).
4. About DSCE: Historical background, MGVP Trust, VTU autonomy, and Chairman's desk.
5. Academics / Courses: Core undergraduate B.E. branches (CSE, ISE, ECE, Aero, Biotech) and postgraduate/research centres.
6. Admissions & Fee Structure: KCET, COMEDK, and Management Quota breakdown with eligibility and annual fees.
7. Placements & Recruiters: CTC statistics (Highest ₹57 LPA, CS avg ₹8-10 LPA) and recruiter network.
8. Campus Life Gallery: 6 campus photographs (Main Block, Central Library, Aerial View, Auditorium, Sports Arena, Student Innovation).
9. Notable Alumni: Kichcha Sudeep, Nosthush Kenjige, and Sunil Abraham.
10. Student Academic Records: Semester examination register table with SGPA and merit classification.
